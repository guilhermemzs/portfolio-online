About me

A journey shaped by education, management, and engineering

I am a Civil Engineering student at Universidade FUMEC and an IT technician trained at COTEMIG. I have worked in education for several years, particularly in English teaching and in the pedagogical and logistics organization of Help School, where I closely support routines, people, and processes.

This combination of experiences allowed me to develop a structured approach to management, communication, and problem-solving early on. I apply these skills every day and intend to bring them into engineering by combining organization, technology, and purpose in every project.

I am interested in engineering, management, technology, education, processes, and leadership, and I believe continuous learning is the path to responsible growth and a solid, meaningful career.
